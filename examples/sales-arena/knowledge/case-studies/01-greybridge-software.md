# Case Study — Greybridge Software

> **Industry.** B2B logistics SaaS · **Headcount.** ~280
> **Reps.** 38 · **CRM.** Salesforce · **Tier.** Pro

## The before

Greybridge's RevOps lead, **Imelda Carrasco**, was hand-stitching the
quarterly forecast from three Tableau dashboards every Monday and
losing 4-6 hours a week to a process the CFO didn't trust. The
rep-rollup forecast came in at `±21%` accuracy on the trailing four
quarters; the CFO had stopped citing it in board materials.

## What changed

Greybridge installed the Suite on a 90-day calibration window in Q1
2025. The Pro tier connectors lit up Salesforce + Google Workspace +
Slack outbound webhooks. The Competitive Intelligence module flagged
a buyer's job posting two days before the rep would have learned
about it organically.

## The measured outcome

After four quarters of calibration, Greybridge reports their
forecast-confidence interval narrowed from `±21%` to `±9%`. They
publish the interval to the CFO's board pack monthly; Imelda no
longer hand-stitches the rollup.

## Quotable

> *"The Suite gave me my Mondays back."* — Imelda Carrasco, RevOps
> Lead, Greybridge Software

## Why this story matters

Greybridge is the canonical *"my BI tool wasn't enough"* story. Reps
working a similar prospect — RevOps-led, BI-tool-already-in-place,
forecast-trust eroding — should reference this case study's
`±21 → ±9` calibration narrowing.
