# Case Study — Vaughn Publishing Solutions

> **Industry.** Publishing-tech (CMS + ad-stack) · **Headcount.**
> ~150 · **Reps.** 22 · **CRM.** Pipedrive · **Tier.** Starter →
> Pro after one quarter

## The before

Vaughn's pipeline was Pipedrive; their RevOps was a single analyst,
**Cheryl Yi**, who'd left their previous role specifically because
the forecast was a daily firefight. She joined Vaughn three weeks
before they signed a paid POC with the Suite, on the explicit
condition that the Suite's calibration window would tell her in 90
days whether the analytics-led forecast or the rep rollup was more
accurate.

## What changed

The Suite's Pipedrive connector (operator-configured, not managed)
took a single working day to wire up. Cheryl led the install. After
the 90-day calibration, the Suite forecast was within ±6% of the
quarter's actual closed-won; the rep rollup was at ±18%.

## The measured outcome

Vaughn moved to the Pro tier immediately. Cheryl is now their VP
RevOps; her case study is the one she gives to prospects who ask
about Pipedrive specifically.

> Publishing Solutions

The Pipedrive-customer story. Most of the Suite's reference logos are
