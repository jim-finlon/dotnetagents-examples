# Case Study — Zafiro Retail Analytics

> **Industry.** Retail-analytics SaaS (POS data) · **Headcount.**
> ~75 · **Reps.** 12 · **CRM.** HubSpot · **Tier.** Starter

## The before

Zafiro's sales team was small but the deal velocity was high
(median 38 days). Their RevOps was a half-time role split with the
finance team, run by **Faustine LeBlanc**. The forecast question
wasn't about confidence intervals — it was about *which deal would
close this week*.

## What changed

Zafiro installed Starter tier on Google Workspace + HubSpot. The
*today's risk list* view became Faustine's morning routine. The
high-velocity deal mix meant the engine's calibration window
completed in 60 days (faster than the 90-day default; the engine
adapts when there's enough closed-won data to anchor the model).

## The measured outcome

Faustine reports that the Suite's weekly close-rate prediction is
within ±2 deals of actual across the trailing 12 weeks. Their CEO
no longer asks her to recompute the weekly forecast by hand on
Fridays.

## Quotable

> *"I get my Friday afternoon back."* — Faustine LeBlanc, RevOps,
> Zafiro Retail Analytics

## Why this story matters

The fast-cycle, small-team Starter-tier story. Reps working a
sub-25-rep prospect with a fast deal cycle (< 60 days median)
should reach for Zafiro — the Suite calibrates faster on
high-velocity pipelines and the lift is real even on the Starter
tier.
