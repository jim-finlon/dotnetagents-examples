# Case Study — Meridian Cybersecurity

> **Industry.** Cybersecurity (SOC + MDR services) · **Headcount.**
> ~95 · **Reps.** 14 · **CRM.** HubSpot · **Tier.** Starter

## The before

Meridian's revenue team was founder-led; their CEO, **Reza Nazari**,
ran the forecast off a single spreadsheet and a gut feel for whether
the security buyers were "actually buying or just shopping." Their
deal cycle was bimodal — fast 30-day deals to mid-market customers
and 9-month enterprise deals to regulated industries.

## What changed

Meridian took the Starter tier on month-to-month — Reza didn't want
to commit to an annual without seeing whether the Suite could
distinguish the two deal-cycle modes. The Suite calibrated each
cohort separately (HubSpot's deal-stage data made this clean) and
produced two forecast bands instead of one.

## The measured outcome

Reza reports that he stopped over-counting the long-cycle enterprise
deals in his quarterly commit. The fast-cycle deals were already
well-forecast by his gut; the long-cycle deals were what was
distorting the rollup. He moved to the Pro tier in quarter two.

## Quotable

> *"The Suite didn't tell me what to do. It told me what I was
> already getting wrong."* — Reza Nazari, CEO, Meridian Cybersecurity

## Why this story matters

The founder-led Starter-tier-first conversion story. Reps working a
sub-25-rep prospect with a CEO doing the forecast should reach for
this case study; the month-to-month Starter tier is the entry point
and the conversion to Pro is the second-quarter conversation.
