# Case Study — Okonkwo Financial Software

> **Industry.** Fintech (treasury + cash-management SaaS) ·
> **Headcount.** ~210 · **Reps.** 31 · **CRM.** Salesforce ·
> **Tier.** Enterprise (EU residency)

## The before

Okonkwo sells into banks. The deal cycle is long (12-18 months) and
security review is the gating step on every deal. Their VP Sales,
**Bayo Okonkwo** (the founder's son; the company is family-led),
needed forecast accuracy that could survive a deal slipping by a
quarter without invalidating the quarterly board commit.

## What changed

Okonkwo took the Enterprise tier with EU residency (their tenant
data lives in Frankfurt; they sell across UK + EU + a small Nordic
book). The Suite's multi-quarter forecasting roadmap item (Q4) was
the deciding factor — Bayo wanted next-quarter + next-quarter+1
visibility, not just current-quarter.

## The measured outcome

Okonkwo's current-quarter forecast interval moved from `±17%` to
`±10%` in three quarters. They're a multi-quarter beta customer for
the Q4 roadmap feature. Bayo serves as the European reference logo
for the Suite — happy to take reference calls from regulated-
industry prospects within EU jurisdictions.

## Quotable

> *"Banks don't buy from companies whose forecasts they don't
> trust. The Suite gave us a number we could defend."* — Bayo
> Okonkwo, VP Sales, Okonkwo Financial Software

## Why this story matters

The regulated-industry + EU-residency story. Reps working an EU
prospect or a financial-services prospect should reach for
Okonkwo first. The case study explicitly mentions the residency
contract — useful for the data-privacy objection conversation.
