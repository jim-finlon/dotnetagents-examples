# Case Study — Stratham Logistics

> **Industry.** Freight + supply-chain SaaS · **Headcount.** ~410
> **Reps.** 62 · **CRM.** Salesforce · **Tier.** Enterprise

## The before

Stratham was the largest Salesforce-rollup org we'd ever shipped to.
Their CRO, **Yumiko Achebe**, had three regional VPs each running
their own pivot tables and disagreeing on every quarterly commit.
The Series B CFO had asked for an independent forecast lens that
didn't depend on any single VP's optimism level.

## What changed

Stratham took the Enterprise tier with custom data residency
(Frankfurt; their EU revenue is ~40% of total). The Suite produced
a region-cohort comparison view that surfaced where the three VPs
disagreed with the engine — and where they disagreed with each
other.

## The measured outcome

Quarter-over-quarter, the Suite's forecast confidence interval moved
from `±22%` to `±9%`. Yumiko cites the case study elsewhere as
"the day we stopped having three forecasts." The three regional VPs
still produce rollups but the board pack uses the Suite's number.

## Quotable

> *"My job got smaller and my forecast got tighter. I'll take
> that trade."* — Yumiko Achebe, CRO, Stratham Logistics

## Why this story matters

The canonical "Enterprise scale + multi-region + multiple regional
VPs disagreeing" case. Reps working a prospect with > 50 reps and
multiple regional sales leaders should lead with this story.
