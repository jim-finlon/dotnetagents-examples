# Competitive Intelligence Module

The CI module is a Pro-tier add-on that watches publicly available
signals about prospects' likely vendor moves. It is opt-in per
customer; the Starter tier ships without it.

## What it watches

- Public job-board postings that name competing vendors.
- Press releases and SEC filings mentioning sales-stack changes.
- Public Glassdoor and LinkedIn signals indicating org changes in the
  buyer's revenue team.
- The buyer's own public marketing changes (pricing-page redesigns,
  product-launch announcements) that often correlate with vendor
  evaluations.

The module does **not** scrape private data, does not bypass
robots.txt, and does not aggregate any signal that requires
authentication to read.

## How it surfaces

When the CI module flags a signal for an active deal, the deal record
gets a sidebar tile with: the signal source, the signal date, and a
one-sentence "what this typically means" written by Mitch & Murray
research staff. Reps see the tile when they open the deal; managers
see a daily roll-up.

## What reps do with it

Three common moves:

1. Reframe the discovery question. *"I noticed Greybridge posted a
   senior RevOps role this morning. Are you evaluating tools as part
   of that hire?"*
2. Time the proposal. *"You announced your Series C yesterday — do
   you want the proposal before or after the quarterly board
   meeting?"*
3. Identify the new champion. *"Your former champion left for
   Whitstable Software last week — who's running the evaluation now?"*

## Honest claim

The CI module raises signal volume; it does not raise win rate by
itself. The case studies that show win-rate lift attribute most of it
to *what the rep did with the signal*, not to the signal itself.
Buyers do not want to feel surveilled. Reps need coaching on how to
reference a signal naturally; the module ships a short play-by-play
guide for first-month enablement.
