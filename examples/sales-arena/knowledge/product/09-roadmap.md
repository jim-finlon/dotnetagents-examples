# Roadmap

Public roadmap, refreshed quarterly. Reps can share the headline
items with prospects; the per-feature timing is directional and not a
contractual commitment.

## This quarter (Q2)

- **Multi-currency forecast**. The forecast view supports a single
  reporting currency today; multi-currency for Pro+ ships this
  quarter.
- **Slack + Teams two-way commenting**. Reps can comment on a
  `dna_slip_risk = high` flag from Slack or Teams; the comment lands
  on the deal record.
- **Improved calibration speed**. Faster calibration for Pro
  customers (the 45-day window) is the headline shipping this
  quarter; technical work is the cross-customer prior.

## Next quarter (Q3)

- **Per-rep pricing experiment**. We are evaluating a per-rep tier
  for very small teams (sub-10 reps). Pricing not finalized.
- **Stretch: Marketo / Pardot signal flow**. MAP signals via direct
  integration rather than CRM-mediated; targeted as a stretch goal
  for the quarter, not a hard commit.
- **Dialer integration**. Call-event ingestion direct from dialer
  APIs (Aircall, Dialpad, Five9). Stretch; capacity-dependent.

## Q4

- **Multi-quarter forecasting**. The Suite forecasts the current
  quarter today; Q4 ships multi-quarter (next-quarter + next-quarter+1)
  forecasting with degraded confidence intervals after the second
  quarter out.
- **Anomaly alerts**. Per-deal anomaly detection (e.g., "this deal's
  engagement pattern looks unlike every prior closed-won deal of its
  size").

## Out of scope for the foreseeable future

- Outbound dialer. The Suite reads call logs, does not place calls.
- Conversational intelligence (Gong-style call transcription +
  scoring). Different category; we partner rather than build.
- Generative-AI sales coaching. The Suite ships *coaching prompts*
  for managers; we deliberately do not ship rep-facing generative
  coaching. The hypothesis is that this is the manager's job.

## Customer roadmap influence

Pro and Enterprise customers can submit and vote on roadmap proposals
through the Pro+ customer portal. Customer-driven feature requests
that pass the quarterly review get a public-roadmap ticket with a
target quarter; the Suite does not commit to a release date.
