# Team and Funding

Mitch & Murray Analytics is the fictional company that ships the
Mitch & Murray Analytics Suite. The team and funding details below
are invented for demo purposes.

## Founders

- **Annaleigh Park** — CEO. Previously RevOps lead at two B2B SaaS
  companies (~$120M ARR at exit). Built the company because she got
  tired of writing the same Monday-morning pivot table by hand.
- **Diego Reyes** — CTO. Previously staff data engineer at a
  CPG-analytics company. Owns the analytics engine architecture.
- **Hannelore Voss** — VP Customer Success. Joined from a customer.
  Half the customer-facing team reports to her.

## Headcount

72 people as of last public update. Distribution:

- Engineering: 28
- Product + design: 9
- Sales + customer success: 18
- Marketing: 6
- Finance + ops + legal: 7
- Executive: 4

## Funding history

- **Seed** ($4M, 2023). Lead: Ridgeline Capital. Participants:
  three angel former-RevOps operators.
- **Series A** ($14M, 2024). Lead: Belmore Ventures. Participants:
  Ridgeline (pro-rata), two new strategic angels.
- **Series B** (closing now per the press release dated last quarter).
  Size: $32M. Lead: Whitstable Growth Partners. The press release is
  public; the use of funds is "platform expansion + EU GTM."

Total raised to date: $50M including the closing Series B.

## Customer counts (public)

- ~180 paying customers across Starter / Pro / Enterprise.
- ~60% North America, ~30% EU, ~10% other.
- Median customer size: 45 reps.
- Public reference customer logos: 12 (case studies under
  [`../case-studies/`](../case-studies/)).

## Why this matters in a sales conversation

The funding + team page is the slide reps reach for when the prospect's
executive asks *"are you going to be here in two years?"* The honest
answer:

- Series B closing means 24+ months of runway at current burn.
- Customer count growing year-over-year.
- No public revenue figure; we don't publish ARR.

## Honest constraints

- The Series B press release is the public artifact; we do not share
  cap-table details.
- We do not publish churn or net revenue retention numbers.
- We do not name customers without their explicit logo-use approval;
  reference calls are gated through Hannelore's team.
