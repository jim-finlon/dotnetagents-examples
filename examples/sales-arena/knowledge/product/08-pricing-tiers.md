# Pricing Tiers

The Suite ships in three tiers. Each tier name maps to a concrete
deal-pack the proposal templates pull from.

## Starter — $1,500 / month

- Up to 25 reps.
- One CRM connector (Salesforce or HubSpot).
- One email-signal connector (Google Workspace or Microsoft 365).
- Analytics engine + pipeline-intelligence dashboard.
- Forecast view with `±25%` confidence interval until calibration
  completes (90 days).
- Standard support: email, business hours.
- Month-to-month, no annual commit required.

Best for: founder-led RevOps, sub-25-rep teams running their first
proper pipeline analytics.

## Pro — $5,500 / month

Everything in Starter, plus:

- Up to 100 reps.
- Multiple CRM + email connectors.
- Competitive Intelligence module.
- BI tool embeds (Power BI / Tableau / Looker).
- Outbound webhooks (Slack, Teams, PagerDuty, custom).
- Operator-configurable cohort comparison.
- Calibration window halved to 45 days (the engine seeds from the
  Suite's anonymized cross-customer prior).
- Priority support: 4-hour business-day response.
- Quarterly commit, 5% prepaid discount.

Best for: 25-100-rep B2B SaaS, mid-market revenue orgs that already
own a BI tool.

## Enterprise — Quote

Everything in Pro, plus:

- Unlimited reps.
- Custom CRM connector (data-warehouse mode).
- Custom data residency.
- Dedicated tenant + customer-managed encryption keys.
- Optional message-body indexing (opt-in with documented retention).
- Custom SLAs, 24/7 incident response for `dna_slip_risk = high`
  flags on deals above an operator-defined size threshold.
- Annual commit, multi-year discounts available.
- Quarterly business review with a Mitch & Murray analyst.

Best for: 100+ reps, regulated industries (financial services, public
sector via partner), customers who need a contracted DPA with custom
terms.

## What we don't do on pricing

- No volume-only discounts. The tier is the discount; bigger commitments
  unlock the longer tier, not a percentage off.
- No "free for nonprofits" tier (we don't carry one).
- No usage-based metering. The Suite is per-tier flat; we lose money
  on heavy customers and break even on light ones, by design.
