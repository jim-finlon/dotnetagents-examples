# Mitch & Murray Analytics Suite — Overview

Mitch & Murray Analytics Suite is a sales-analytics SaaS for B2B revenue
teams who want to know which deals are actually moving and which look
like they're moving but aren't. The Suite sits between the CRM and the
exec dashboard, watches every deal-level signal across email, calendar,
pipeline state changes, and proposal-acceptance events, and answers
three questions every Monday morning:

1. Which deals will sign this quarter and which are stalling?
2. Which rep tactics are working this quarter and which aren't?
3. Where is forecast risk hiding inside the official commit?

The product is built for sales managers, RevOps leads, and revenue-side
founders running a sub-200-rep team. It is **not** a CRM. It does not
own the deal record. It reads from whatever CRM the customer already
runs and writes back only the analytics outputs the customer chooses
to surface.

## Three-word positioning

*"Forecast risk visible."*

## What it isn't

- A CRM replacement.
- A coaching platform (the Suite tells the manager what to coach; it
  does not run the conversation).
- An autopilot. Every recommendation routes through a human-in-the-loop
  approval surface.

## Who it's for

Sales managers and RevOps leaders at 20-200-rep B2B software companies
with a deal-cycle median in the 30-180 day band. Outside that band the
fit weakens — transactional sub-30-day cycles get less lift from
pipeline analytics; > 180-day enterprise cycles need a different
calibration the Suite does not currently ship.

## Founding intent

The Suite was built by ex-RevOps practitioners who got tired of writing
the same Monday-morning pivot table by hand every week. The product is
that pivot table, with a feedback loop, with an opinion. It opinions
toward *which deal is at risk* over *which rep is performing* — the
hypothesis is that deal-level risk catches reps in time to coach;
rep-level performance only catches them after the quarter closes.
