# FAQ — Pricing

## What does it cost?

Starter $1,500/mo · Pro $5,500/mo · Enterprise quote. Full breakdown
at [`product/08-pricing-tiers.md`](../product/08-pricing-tiers.md).

## What's the commitment?

Starter: month-to-month, no annual required.
Pro: quarterly commit with a 5% prepaid discount; month-to-month
also available at standard pricing.
Enterprise: annual commit with multi-year discounts available.

## Is there a per-seat fee?

No. Pricing is flat per tier. The tier caps the rep count (25 for
Starter, 100 for Pro, unlimited for Enterprise). Per-seat models are
on the [`roadmap`](../product/09-roadmap.md) as an experiment for
sub-10-rep teams.

## Is there a setup fee?

No, except for the optional Enterprise-tier paid implementation
engagement (custom CRM via warehouse-mode), which is fixed-fee, 5
business days.

## Is there a usage limit?

No usage metering. The Suite is per-tier flat. Heavy users do not
incur additional cost.

## Do you offer discounts?

- 5% prepaid quarterly discount on Pro.
- Multi-year discounts on Enterprise.
- No nonprofit / startup / educational discounts (we don't ship a
  free tier and don't carry one).
- No volume-only discounts. Tier change is the discount mechanism.

## Can I downgrade?

Yes, at any commit boundary. Starter → cancel month-to-month with
no penalty. Pro → revert to Starter at the next quarterly
boundary. Enterprise → revert to Pro at the next annual boundary.

## Can I pay in EUR or GBP?

Yes; EU and UK tenants are billed in their local currency at the
quarterly mid-market rate.

## What's in / out of the Pro tier?

In: everything in Starter, multiple connectors, Competitive
Intelligence module, BI embeds, outbound webhooks, faster
calibration (45 days). Out: custom data residency, customer-managed
keys, custom CRM via warehouse, optional body-indexing, 24/7
incident response — those are Enterprise-tier features.

## Do you negotiate?

On Enterprise, yes — commit length, multi-year, custom DPA terms,
optional implementation engagement. On Pro and Starter, the
sticker price is the price.
