# FAQ — General

## What is the Mitch & Murray Analytics Suite?

A sales-analytics SaaS that watches deal-level signals across the
customer's CRM, email metadata, and calendar metadata, and produces
per-deal slip-risk scores plus a calibration-history-backed quarterly
forecast confidence interval. See
[`product/01-overview.md`](../product/01-overview.md).

## Who is it for?

Sales managers and RevOps leaders at 20-200-rep B2B companies with a
30-180-day median deal cycle. Outside that band the fit weakens.

## Does it replace my CRM?

No. The Suite reads from the CRM and writes analytics outputs back
as custom fields. The customer's CRM remains the system of record.

## Does it replace my BI tool?

No. The Suite ships with a hosted dashboard and Power BI / Tableau /
Looker embeds. Customers typically keep their BI tool and use the
Suite as a feed.

## Does it score reps?

No, by design. The Suite scores *deals*, not *reps*. Rep-level
scoring is what kills adoption in this category; deal-level scoring
shows the manager which deal to ask about.

## Is it an autopilot?

No. Every signal the engine emits routes through a human-in-the-loop
approval surface. The Suite suggests; the manager decides.

## How long does setup take?

Day-one for managed Salesforce + Google Workspace or HubSpot +
Microsoft 365 connectors. 5-15 business days for the custom-CRM
warehouse-mode connector on Enterprise tier.

## What if our CRM is custom?

See [`product/06-integrations.md`](../product/06-integrations.md)
under "Custom CRM via warehouse" — Enterprise-tier feature; takes
5-15 business days. Reference: Fenwick Industrial case study.
