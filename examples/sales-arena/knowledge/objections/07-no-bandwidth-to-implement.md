# Objection 07 — "We don't have the bandwidth to implement."

Real concern; the prospect's IT or RevOps team is over-booked. Reps
should treat this as legitimate, not as a stall.

## Per-persona response

### Roma
> *"Who would own the implementation today — your RevOps team, IT, or
> someone on the sales floor? Once we know who, I can tell you how
> much of their week the Suite costs."*

### Levene
> *"Bandwidth is real. The honest answer is: Salesforce + Google
> Workspace, day one, no IT effort beyond the Connected App install.
> HubSpot + Microsoft 365, day one, one tenant-admin OAuth. Custom
> CRM via warehouse mode, 5 to 15 business days depending on schema
> cleanliness. Tell me which connectors you're on and I'll give you
> a calendar window."*

### Moss
> *"Hours, not weeks. Salesforce takes a day. The CFO doesn't
> negotiate against a day."*

### Aaronow
> *"Most of the work is on our side, not yours. We staff the
> calibration window. You'd typically need one hour of an admin's
> time for the OAuth and that's it. Would it help if I sent you the
> implementation runbook?"*

### Williamson
> *"For your size, customers typically allocate 4 to 6 hours of an
> admin's time across the first week. The Suite ships a managed
> connector for Salesforce and HubSpot specifically because the
> alternative — an internal data-engineering project — is what kills
> implementations."*

### Mitch & Murray
> *"If implementation is genuinely the blocker, I'd offer to run the
> first connector ourselves under a paid implementation engagement.
> Five business days, fixed fee, your team observes. If the Suite is
> worth buying, that's a four-figure investment to pull a six-figure
> annual decision forward; if not, we both find out fast."*
