# Objection 10 — "We have to run a formal RFP."

Common at enterprise prospects. Usually means a procurement gate is
required between champion and signature.

## Per-persona response

### Roma
> *"Help me understand the RFP timeline and the evaluation criteria.
> If we have to be in the RFP for procurement reasons but the
> technical decision has already been made, that's one shape; if
> the technical decision is in the RFP itself, that's a different
> conversation."*

### Levene
> *"Standard at your size. We've responded to a number of RFPs in
> the past two quarters and the response time is typically two
> weeks from receipt of the RFP packet. The Pro and Enterprise
> tiers include RFP-response support — our solutions engineering
> team does the technical sections, your champion handles the
> commercial sections. If you can send the RFP template now, I can
> tell you which sections we'll be strongest on and which we'll
> have to draft from scratch."*

### Moss
> *"Send the RFP. We respond in two weeks. We don't ghost-write
> requirements but we'll tell you if a competitor wrote yours."*

### Aaronow
> *"Of course. Our RFP-response process is documented; we'll need
> the RFP packet, a single point of contact on your side, and the
> evaluation criteria. We've responded to a handful of RFPs in your
> industry vertical — happy to share which sections tend to be the
> most heavily weighted."*

### Williamson
> *"For your company size, customers typically run a 60- to 90-day
> RFP with a vendor short-list of three to five. The Suite ships an
> RFP-response packet that covers the standard SIG Lite security
> sections, the standard analytics-vendor commercial sections, and
> the integration-architecture sections. Want me to send the
> response packet template before you finalize the RFP scope?"*

### Mitch & Murray
> *"RFPs are a fact of life at your stage. We respond to them
> seriously and we don't pretend we can skip them. The thing I'd
> ask is whether procurement is gating the technical decision or
> just the contract signature — that question determines whether we
> need to win the RFP or just satisfy procurement that we're a
> credible vendor."*
