# Objection 02 — "We already have a vendor for this."

Usually means the prospect runs a BI tool, a CRM dashboard layer, or
a homegrown spreadsheet pivot. Rarely means a direct Suite competitor;
when it does, route to [`competitors/`](../competitors/).

## Per-persona response

### Roma
> *"Help me understand what your current vendor told you they'd give
> you — and then tell me whether they delivered it. The gap between
> those two answers is usually where we have a conversation worth
> having."*

### Levene
> *"That's actually how most of our customers found us. They were
> running Salesforce reports or a Power BI dashboard their analyst
> built, and the analyst left or the reports got out of date or the
> exec said 'I don't trust this forecast anymore.' We're not
> replacing the BI tool — we ride on top of it. The integrations
> page lists exactly which connectors we offer."*

### Moss
> *"What does the vendor charge you and what is the forecast
> accuracy?"*

### Aaronow
> *"I don't want to displace anything that's working. Could you walk
> me through what the current tool does well and what it doesn't?
> If the Suite overlaps, I'd rather know now and stop the
> conversation."*

### Williamson
> *"What customers in your size band tell us is that they keep the
> BI tool — Power BI or Tableau — and use the Suite as a feed into
> it. The decision usually isn't 'replace,' it's 'add a feed that
> our existing tool can render.'"*

### Mitch & Murray
> *"The two questions an executive asks are: does the existing tool
> give you a per-deal slip-risk score, and does it write a
> calibration-history-backed forecast confidence interval. If the
> answer to either is no, the Suite is adjacent, not redundant. I'd
> rather not push if both answers are yes."*
