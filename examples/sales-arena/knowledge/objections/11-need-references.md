# Objection 11 — "I need references before we move forward."

Reasonable ask. The Suite ships 12 public reference logos and a
gated reference-call process.

## Per-persona response

### Roma
> *"What kind of reference would actually move the needle — a
> customer your size, in your industry, with your CRM, or all three?
> The more specific you can be about the comparable, the more
> useful the call will be."*

### Levene
> *"Reference calls are a healthy part of the buying process and
> we don't try to short-circuit them. We have 12 public logos in
> the case-studies folder and our Customer Success team gates the
> call list — typically two reference calls per prospect, scheduled
> within 10 business days of your request. Tell me what kind of
> reference would matter and I'll route the request to Hannelore's
> team."*

### Moss
> *"What industry, what size, what CRM. I'll come back with two
> names by Friday."*

### Aaronow
> *"Sure. I can send the case-study pack today — that gives you the
> public stories. For a live call, I'll need to coordinate through
> our Customer Success team; usually a week to two weeks to schedule
> depending on the reference's calendar. Which case study from the
> pack would you want to talk to first?"*

### Williamson
> *"Customers in your band usually want a reference of similar size
> running the same CRM. From our public logo list, two that match
> your profile are in the case-studies folder; happy to gate-keep a
> call to either."*

### Mitch & Murray
> *"References are a fair ask. Our Customer Success team manages
> the gating because a reference customer is a real person whose
> time we don't burn. Two calls is the standard ask; if you need
> more for the procurement gate, we make that happen but with
> longer scheduling lead times. What's the timing pressure?"*
