# Objection 08 — "We have security concerns."

Almost always real. Route the conversation to
[`product/07-security-and-compliance.md`](../product/07-security-and-compliance.md)
and offer the security pack as the first artifact.

## Per-persona response

### Roma
> *"What specifically — is it data residency, the SOC 2 question, or
> a custom security review? Each of those gets a different artifact;
> tell me which and I'll send the right one."*

### Levene
> *"We get the security pack on the table in the first call when we
> can. SOC 2 Type II current, ISO 27001 current, GDPR DPA available,
> data residency US-east, US-west, EU-Frankfurt, EU-Dublin. Custom
> security questionnaires target five business days from receipt.
> Want me to send the full pack tonight?"*

### Moss
> *"Send me your questionnaire. We'll have it back in five business
> days. SOC 2 Type II is current."*

### Aaronow
> *"Of course. I'll email you the security one-pager and the SOC 2
> report under NDA today. If there's a specific clause your security
> team wants to discuss, I can route to our CISO directly — she
> tends to do that call herself."*

### Williamson
> *"For your size band, customers typically run the Suite through a
> standard vendor-risk review. Our security pack covers the SIG
> Lite, the CAIQ, and the standard vendor-risk questionnaire fields.
> The custom-residency option on Enterprise tier covers the more
> unusual requirements."*

### Mitch & Murray
> *"Security is the first technical question on every executive call
> and I'd rather get it out of the way. SOC 2 Type II audited
> annually, third-party pen test annual, responsible-disclosure
> program live at our security URL. We sign DPAs and MNDAs; we do
> not sign BAAs and we do not pretend to be HIPAA-compliant. Where
> does your security review live in the buying cycle?"*
