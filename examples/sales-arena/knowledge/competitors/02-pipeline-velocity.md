# Competitor — Pipeline Velocity (invented)

> *The modern competitor.* The startup that pitches similar
> calibration math to ours.

## Their pitch

Pipeline Velocity is a 4-year-old Series A company with a similar
analytics-engine + forecast-confidence story. They market explicitly
on AI-driven prediction and ship a slick demo experience. Their
target customer is sub-50-rep SaaS — same as our Starter and lower
Pro tier.

## Where they win

- **Onboarding speed.** Their managed Salesforce connector is faster
  than ours (they ship in hours; we ship in a day). Real difference,
  not pretend.
- **AI / ML positioning.** They market the prediction model
  aggressively; some buyers find their pitch deck more compelling
  than ours.
- **Sub-25-rep pricing.** They ship a per-rep tier at $79/rep/mo
  that catches teams smaller than our Starter cap.

## Where we win

- **Calibration transparency.** Pipeline Velocity ships a black-box
  model; the Suite ships the methodology one-pager and the per-deal
  reason codes. RevOps leads care about this.
- **Data residency.** We ship EU residency on Enterprise; Pipeline
  Velocity is US-only.
- **Security posture.** SOC 2 Type II + ISO 27001 + GDPR DPA. They
  have SOC 2 Type I; the Type II rollout is on their roadmap.
- **Real-deal-cycle calibration window.** Our 90-day Starter and
  45-day Pro windows are publicly documented. Pipeline Velocity
  claims first-day accuracy; our case studies (notably
  [`../case-studies/04-meridian-cybersecurity.md`](../case-studies/04-meridian-cybersecurity.md))
  show why that claim breaks under real data.

## Where we lose

- Founder-led teams under 25 reps who want the fastest possible
  install. Pipeline Velocity wins those.
- Prospects who want a slicker demo over a more honest pitch.
- Prospects who don't read security pack details.

## Pricing posture

Pipeline Velocity is roughly two-thirds the price of the Suite at
comparable rep counts. They make the per-rep math work by skipping
the calibration window — they bill on day one.

## Honest framing for reps

> *"Pipeline Velocity is a real competitor and they win some deals
> they should win. If the prospect prioritizes time-to-first-dashboard
> over forecast-confidence interval, Pipeline Velocity is the right
> answer. If they prioritize accuracy that survives the calibration
> window, we are."*
