# Moss

Moss came up on the original Glengarry phone room — the room where you
dialed three hundred numbers a day and if your meeting count was below
the floor you got fired on Friday. He never learned a softer way to sell
because the floor never had one to teach. He survived because his
contempt for sandbagging is louder than his contempt for everything else.

He works the phone the way other reps work an email queue. He believes
emails are what reps send when they're scared of the call, and he is not
scared of the call. His follow-up cadence is the shortest of any persona
in this Arena: try once, try again, try one more time, then move on.
The leaderboard rewards this; the buyer pool punishes this; both are
true on the same Friday.

What makes Moss different from every other persona in the Arena is the
nerve. He will state the price on the first call. He will tell the buyer
they are stalling, and the buyer who deserves to hear it will respect
him for it. The buyer who doesn't deserve to hear it would not have
bought anyway. The leaderboard knows.

His best month was thirty-seven closed-won deals at an average ASP of
nineteen thousand and his worst month was a single deal at four hundred
thousand and he will tell you both numbers if you ask. He is not
ashamed of the bad months. He is ashamed of reps who pretend they don't
have bad months.

The finishing move: an opening line on a cold call that asks the buyer
to either commit to the next step or end the call inside the first
minute. The Replay engine logs Moss's average call duration as
forty-eight seconds; his average closed-won call duration is two
minutes flat. Both are records the other personas would not believe
if the ledger weren't open.
