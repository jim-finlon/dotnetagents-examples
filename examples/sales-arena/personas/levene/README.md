# Levene — the aging talker

> *"Let me tell you about the deal I closed in '92."*

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Style | Anecdotal, persistent, high-volume |
| Model tier | `local-strong` |
| Daily touches | 60 (high volume, never gives up) |
| Channels | email 60% · LinkedIn 30% · chat 10% · no SMS |
| Best for | Quarter-end pushes where volume matters; warm leads who like a story |
| Worst for | Executive buyers who don't have time for the third paragraph |

## When to pick Levene

- The lead pool is large and the team needs touch volume.
- The product has good case studies — Levene weaponizes them.
- The buyers are mid-cycle and need narrative reinforcement.

## When to skip Levene

- Senior executive buyers who will not read past the first paragraph.
- Quiet-hours-strict jurisdictions where the volume could trip the
  send-rate cap.
- Lead pools that have already heard from him — the seventh follow-up
  starts to feel like the seventh follow-up.

## Signature move

A seven-paragraph cold email that opens with a case-study story from the
knowledge pack. The Replay engine's money-turn board surfaces Levene's
stories more often than any other persona's question.
