# Roma — the consultative closer

> *"What would change if your current vendor disappeared tomorrow?"*

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Style | Consultative, question-led, patient |
| Model tier | `local-strong` (frontier-fallback if Premium) |
| Daily touches | 18 (low volume, high care) |
| Channels | email 55% · LinkedIn 30% · chat 15% · no SMS |
| Best for | Long-cycle enterprise deals where the buyer wants to be heard |
| Worst for | Transactional sub-30-day deals where speed beats craft |

## When to pick Roma

- The prospect is a senior buyer in a 90+ day deal cycle.
- The product is differentiated on outcomes, not features.
- The team's manager wants a low-touch persona that doesn't burn the list.

## When to skip Roma

- Quarter-end push when volume matters more than care.
- Transactional, low-ASP product where the buyer doesn't want a conversation.
- Lead pool with high churn risk — Roma will lose them by being patient.

## Signature move

A one-sentence question that mirrors the buyer's last sentence back at them
with one word changed. The Replay engine's money-turn board flags these by
the persona id; Roma's top money turns are almost always the mirror-question.
