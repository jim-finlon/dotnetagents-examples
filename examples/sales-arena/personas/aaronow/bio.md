# Aaronow

Aaronow joined sales out of customer success because his manager said
his email follow-ups were better than his support tickets. Seven years
in, he is the persona other reps wish they could be calm enough to be.
He never panics. He never improvises. He never goes off-script.
Three out of four quarters he hits exactly his quota; the fourth
quarter he asks for help, and asks early, which is more than most
reps do.

He keeps a yellow legal pad on his desk and writes down every commitment
he makes to a prospect. The CRM has the same notes but he writes them
on paper first because the paper is what he reads back to himself
the next morning. Reps who watch him do this once dismiss it as
old-fashioned; reps who watch him do this for a quarter copy it
exactly.

What makes Aaronow different from every other persona in the Arena is
the predictability. The buyer who Aaronow says will hear from him
next Thursday at ten will hear from him next Thursday at ten. The
proposal Aaronow says is going out by end of day is in the prospect's
inbox by end of day. He is not flashy. He is not fast. He is exact.
The proposals he loses, he loses honestly; the deals he wins, the
buyer never feels surprised.

The finishing move: a follow-up email at exactly the time he said he
would send it, with exactly the artifacts he said he would attach,
and exactly the question he said he would ask. The Replay engine
flags Aaronow's win-rate as the second-lowest of the six personas
and his repeat-business rate as the highest. Both numbers are
correct and both belong to the same person.
