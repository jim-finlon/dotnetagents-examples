## When to pick Aaronow

- Buyers value predictability over charisma.
- The deal cycle is procurement-led — Aaronow knows how to honor a slow buy.
- The team wants a persona with the highest repeat-business rate, even at
  the cost of win-rate on the first contract.

## When to skip Aaronow

- Hot leads that need a closer to take the call today.
- Volume pushes where touch count matters more than touch quality.
- Buyers who want to hear an opinion — Aaronow will hedge.

exactly the artifacts he promised. The Replay engine's repeat-business
column shows Aaronow at the top of the persona list every quarter.
