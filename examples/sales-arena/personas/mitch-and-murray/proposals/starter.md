# Starter — executive register

> not work outbound deals.*

## When this template fires

The orchestrator invokes the Mitch & Murray proposal template only when:

- Williamson kicks a Starter-tier discount request upstairs.
- The knowledge pack's executive-perspective objection response (see
  `knowledge/objections/*.md` Mitch & Murray sections) references a
  Starter-tier deal.
- The contest-wrap narration summarizes Starter-tier deals shipped.

## Voice

The Starter-tier proposal in the Mitch & Murray voice is short. Three
sentences. It is the voice of the corner office writing a footnote to a
quarterly board pack, not the voice of a closer writing a pitch.

## Standard block

```
{{prospect.first_name}}, the Starter tier is $1,500/month, month-to-month,
no commit. Calibration window 90 days, forecast confidence interval lands
in quarter one. If it doesn't earn its keep by the calibration window,
cancel without penalty.
```

That is the entire proposal at this tier in this voice. The numbers don't
have feelings about the deal.
