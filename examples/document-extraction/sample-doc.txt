# Acme Corp Customer Support and Escalation Guide

## Section 1: Overview
Welcome to the Acme Corp Customer Support team. Our mission is to provide outstanding service. Support requests are handled through our ticketeer system and categorized by urgency.

## Section 2: Urgency Levels
We define three urgency levels:
1. Urgent (P0): Database locked, server unreachable, or security vulnerability. Initial response target is 15 minutes.
2. Moderate (P1): Performance degradation or feature broken. Initial response target is 2 hours.
3. Low (P2): General questions, billing inquiries, or feature requests. Initial response target is 24 hours.

## Section 3: Escalation Paths
If a P0 issue is not resolved within 1 hour, escalate to the Engineering Lead (Jane Doe) via phone.
For P1 issues not resolved within 4 hours, notify the on-call product lead through the configured team channel.
For billing queries, route directly to the finance channel (#billing-support).
